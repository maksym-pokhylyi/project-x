## 0. Method and limits (read first)

- WebSearch was NOT available in this session (shared search budget exhausted before this analyst started) and the WebFetch tool could not resolve hosts. All live evidence below was therefore obtained by fetching KNOWN primary-source URLs directly (regulator pages, official legal texts via the EU Publications Office / eCFR / govinfo, vendor pricing pages, official open data). No search engine, third-party reader, proxy or web archive was used.
- Consequence: discovery of unknown competitors, forum threads and keyword volumes is weaker than in a normal run. Everything not fetched live in this session is labelled `BACKGROUND (not re-verified)` and comes from the analyst model's prior knowledge (cut-off mid-2026).
- Labels used: FACT (fetched primary source, URL + date), INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION, UNKNOWN, BACKGROUND (not re-verified).

### UK Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD ITSA)
- FACT. Thresholds/dates: qualifying income > GBP 50,000 (2024-25) -> from 6 Apr 2026; > GBP 30,000 (2025-26) -> from 6 Apr 2027; > GBP 20,000 (2026-27) -> from 6 Apr 2028. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-out-if-and-when-you-need-to-use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax (last updated 26 Mar 2026; fetched 2026-11-13).
- FACT. Volumes and costs (HMRC impact note): "around 780,000 people ... from April 2026 with a further 970,000 joining from April 2027"; average transitional cost GBP 320 and average annual additional cost GBP 110 per business (GBP 350 / GBP 110 for the 30-50k cohort). Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/extension-of-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-to-sole-traders-and-landlords/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-for-sole-traders-and-landlords (fetched 2026-11-13).
- FACT. HMRC developer guide (updated 15 Sep 2026) carries the warning: "HMRC is no longer accepting production credential access requests for new 2026-27 quarterly update products, as the market window for these products has now closed." It also sets minimum functionality standards, mandatory fraud-prevention headers (legal requirement), sandbox testing of all endpoints and a Production Approvals Checklist; an "in-year product" (quarterly updates only) is an allowed product type. Source: https://developer.service.hmrc.gov.uk/guides/income-tax-mtd-end-to-end-service-guide/ and .../documentation/how-to-integrate.html (fetched 2026-11-13).
- FACT. HMRC "expects" free products: "The UK government is committed to ensuring the availability of free software products for small businesses with simple tax affairs" (same guide).
- FACT. HMRC's public vendor data file for the software finder lists 184 recognised products; 40 flag a free version (incl. Monzo, Starling, Lloyds Bank, Tide, SumUp, Zoho, Sage free tier, FreeAgent via NatWest/Mettle); 92 are bridging products; niche verticals already present (childminders, pet sitters, therapists, trades, CIS, landlords). Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hmrc/income-tax-software-choices-frontend/main/conf/software-vendors.json (fetched 2026-11-13; file field lastUpdated = 2025-09-19, which may be stale).

### US ADA Title II web/mobile accessibility rule + HHS Section 504
- FACT. DOJ Interim Final Rule published 20 Apr 2026 extended compliance dates: entities with population >= 50,000 -> 26 Apr 2027; < 50,000 and special districts -> 26 Apr 2028. Source: https://www.ada.gov/resources/2024-03-08-web-rule/ and https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-04-20/html/2026-07663.htm (fetched 2026-11-13).

- FACT. HHS Section 504 rule, 45 CFR 84.84 as currently in force (amended 91 FR 25507, 11 May 2026): recipients with >= 15 employees must meet WCAG 2.1 AA from 11 May 2027; < 15 employees from 10 May 2028. Source: eCFR API https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-11-01/title-45.xml?part=84&section=84.84 (fetched 2026-11-13).

- FACT. Art. 9: employers with 250+ workers report by 7 June 2027 and every year; 150-249 workers by 7 June 2027 and every three years; 100-149 workers by 7 June 2031 and every three years. ### EU e-commerce consumer-law triggers (withdrawal button, guarantee notice, GPSR) - plugin market evidence
- FACT. - FACT. WordPress.org plugin directory API (https://api.wordpress.org/plugins/info/1.2/, queried 2026-11-13): search "withdrawal button" returns 296 plugins; the leaders are FREE and already have 6,000+, 5,000+, 2,000+ and 1,000+ active installs; "verifactu" returns 30 plugins (largest 900+ installs, most 0-40, many published Sep-Nov 2026); "ksef" 20 plugins (largest 700+); "gpsr" 36 (largest 300+); "peppol" 41 (largest 100+); "zugferd" 29 results, the two name-matching ones have 0 installs.
