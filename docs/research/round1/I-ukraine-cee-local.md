# I — Ukraine / CEE "home-field" route to $10k MRR (round 1)

Research date: 2026-11-13. Scope: test whether a Ukrainian solo founder's home-field advantages (language, legal presence as ФОП group 3, local payment rails, knowledge of local rules) give a better route to $10k MRR in 12 months than a global English-language B2B product.

---

## 0. Bottom line

**Verdict: no.** No purely local (UAH-priced Ukrainian SMB) or CEE-compliance opportunity found here is a better bet for $10k MRR in 12 months than a USD-priced B2B product sold globally. The home-field advantage is real but pays off in only one shape: **a USD-priced tool whose first 20–40 customers are Ukrainian companies that earn in USD (IT outsourcing/outstaffing firms, agencies), sold in the native language and invoiced locally, and built so the same product sells abroad from month 3–4.** That is a global product with a local beachhead, not a local product.

My subjective odds of reaching $10k MRR by month 12 are 10–15% for the best option below and single digits for the rest.

Four reasons, each resting on facts verified this session:

1. **Local price levels are low.** SMB SaaS list prices cluster at about 105–1,700 UAH/month, roughly $2–37 (Taxer 1,450 UAH/year, Вчасно.Звіт 1,260 UAH/year, Checkbox 348 UAH/month, HugeProfit 349–1,499, SalesDrive 791–991, Poster 600–2,400). At those prices $10k MRR needs roughly 270–4,300 paying customers.
2. **The target is bigger in UAH than the brief assumed.** NBU official rate was 45.50 UAH/USD on 11 Nov 2026, so $10,000 ≈ 455,000 UAH, not 420,000.
3. **Every 2027 "forcing event" has slipped or shrunk.** Mandatory VAT for simplified-system ФОП is off the 2027 calendar (IMF benchmark moved in July 2026: law by end-April 2027, effect January 2028 at the earliest; the tax-committee chair says not before the war ends). е-Акциз moved to 1 July 2027, е-ТТН to 1 June 2027 (bill only approved by the Cabinet), parcel VAT to not earlier than 1 July 2027, SAF-T-for-all-VAT-payers bill withdrawn. All five of those have slipped or been withdrawn at least once; only the platform-reporting law (DAC7) arrived roughly on schedule, and its first report covers 2027.
4. **The state and banks give the basic toolchain away, and incumbents are consolidating.** Дія files ФОП declarations, monobank issues a free e-seal for ПРРО, PrivatBank zeroed tariffs for active ФОП and bundles free reporting/accounting services, the Rada adopted free pre-filling of VAT returns on 11 Nov 2026 (bill 16037-д). Checkbox raised its price 40% on 19 Oct 2026 while Вчасно.Каса advertises a 50%-off or free switching offer; Vchasno Group bought 80% of the ФОП-reporting service ЯТакс in Feb 2025.

**Ranked top 3 (confidence: low to medium, see limits below):**

| # | Opportunity | Buyer / price | Why it ranks here | Kill-risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Proposal-ready CV and bench packaging for IT outstaffing/outsourcing firms (Ukraine beachhead, then global) | Head of sales/delivery; $49–149/month per company; ~125 customers | USD budgets, instant value, no regulatory or accounting liability, language-neutral expansion | Buyers decide a $20 ChatGPT seat plus a Word template is good enough |
| 2 | Prozorro AI bid assistant for SME suppliers | Owner/tender manager; ~990–2,490 UAH/month; ~300 customers | Open API, open contact data for outbound, revenue-linked willingness to pay | Accredited marketplaces ship the same AI features free |
| 3 | ФОП-contractor operations (acts, signatures, payment files, registry monitoring) for firms with 10–300 ФОП contractors | CFO/chief accountant; ~$2 per contractor, $49 minimum; ~70–100 firms | Maximum home-field fit (local rules, local invoicing), mid ARPU | Вчасно/HRIS already cover it; drifts into per-client template work |

Downgraded after analysis: Ukrainian-language KSeF assistant for Ukrainian sole proprietors in Poland (low ARPU, main adoption wave passed in April 2026, payment and invoicing friction), military-registration/reservation compliance (sensitive data, war-dependent), DAC7 kit for small platforms (tiny buyer count, first report only on 2027 data).

### 4.2 Notes per trigger

**Mandatory VAT for simplified-system ФОП.** This was the largest candidate trigger and it is gone from the window.

- [V] Dec 2025: MinFin published a draft requiring VAT registration from 1 Jan 2027 for single-tax payers above 1M UAH of sales in 12 months, 20% rate, with the group-3 single-tax rate falling from 5% to 3% for VAT payers [S11, S12].
- [V] Feb 2026: threshold raised to 4M UAH (about EUR 85k) under the new IMF programme; a second MinFin draft followed [S13, S14].
- [V] 19–20 Apr 2026: PM Svyrydenko said the IMF accepted the idea was "sensitive and non-constructive" and that alternatives would be sought for the 2027 budget; MP Zhelezniak called it postponed rather than cancelled [S15, S16].
- [V] 21 July 2026: IMF first review moved the structural benchmark from April 2026 to April 2027; the law is to take effect from January 2028 [S17, S18, S19].
- [V] 19 Aug 2026: tax-committee chair Hetmantsev said it is postponed and will not be introduced at least until the war ends [S20, S21].
- [UNK] How many ФОП exceed the 1M or 4M threshold. Not captured.
- [INF] Even if a law passes in spring 2027, demand for VAT tooling would begin in late 2027 for a January 2028 start, at the very edge of month 12, and bill 16037-д means the e-cabinet will pre-fill the documents for free. Do not build on this.

**Single-tax reform (groups 2 and 3).**

- [V] The updated IMF memorandum plans a 2027 reform of groups 2 and 3 with rates up to 10% and auto-filling; MinFin is preparing a bill to move some activities out of group 2 and differentiate group-3 rates by activity, with up to 10% for certain services [S19, S22]. MinFin has not said which services.
- [V] Independently of any reform, fixed payments rise in 2027 with the minimum wage: group-2 single tax up to 1,909.20 UAH/month, minimum social contribution 2,100.12 UAH/month; group 3 stays at 5% + 1% military levy (3% for VAT payers) [S27].
- [INF] If services ФОП are pushed into percentage-based taxation, more of them need income tracking, but Taxer, Вчасно.Звіт, Дія and the banks already do that. The reform matters more to companies that pay many ФОП contractors (see opportunity 3).

**е-Акциз.**

- [V] Mandatory e-stamps slipped from 1 Jan 2026 to 1 Nov 2026 (Law 4698), testing was extended to 12 Oct 2026, and the Rada then moved the launch to 1 July 2027 (bill 15532) after business warned of a 6.5bn UAH revenue shortfall; paper stamps remain valid meanwhile [S28, S29, S30]. Date of the vote and signature [UNK].
- [INF] Affects producers, importers and every retailer of alcohol, tobacco and e-liquids. The retail need is scanning at the till, so it lands inside Checkbox, Poster, Вчасно.Каса and the like. No solo wedge. Number of licensed outlets [UNK].

**е-ТТН.**

- [V] Draft law published 9 Apr 2026 with a 1 Jan 2027 start; Cabinet Resolution 720 of 4 June 2026 made the system permanent; the Cabinet-approved bill now expects 1 June 2027 [S31, S32, S33].
- [V] Access runs through connected e-document operators; Вчасно.ТТН and M.E.Doc were first to get full access (Jan 2026) [S34].
- [INF] A solo vendor cannot become an operator in a day; this needs state integration and authorisation. Flagged as a certification-type requirement. The only solo angle would be a thin layer on top of an operator's API, which the operator can copy.

**SAF-T UA.**

- [V] Bill 6255 (all VAT payers from 2027) was withdrawn in July 2025 and no replacement had been filed as of December 2025; large taxpayers already submit, and the tax service published a list of typical file errors in Sept 2026; e-audit started 1 Jan 2026 [S35, S36, S37].
- [INF] Buyers are large taxpayers served by ERP vendors and Big-4 advisers. Not a micro-SaaS market.

**ПРРО and cash registers.**

- [V] MinFin drafted changes to the form and mandatory fields of fiscal receipts that could apply from 1 Jan 2027; still a draft [S52, S53].
- [V] Group-1 ФОП have had to accept electronic payments since 1 Jan 2026 [S54].
- [INF] Receipt-format changes are absorbed by incumbents in a release. No new adoption wave.

**Digital platforms (DAC7), Law 4903-IX.**

- [V] Adopted 9 June 2026 (bill 15111-д), in force 23 Oct 2026. Platform operators must register with the tax service by 1 Jan 2027, collect and report seller data, withhold personal income tax where applicable; first reporting period is 2027 [S38, S39, S42, S44]. Extracts disagree on the signing date (June or October) and one earlier overview gave 1 Nov 2026 as the registration deadline.
- [V] Individuals selling through platforms get a preferential 10% rate up to 834 minimum wages; one extract cites an exemption up to EUR 2,000 a year (described for a bill version; verify in the final text) [S39, S43].
- [V] Deloitte: the law does not change taxation of ФОП or companies, and the exchange rules apply only after Ukraine joins the DPI MCAA [S40]. Sources disagree on whether the operator acts as tax agent [S41].
- [INF] Effect on sellers: for casual sellers on Ukrainian platforms the platform withholds, so compliance becomes automatic and the incentive to register as ФОП falls. For sellers on foreign platforms (Etsy, eBay, Upwork) visibility to the tax service rises only when international exchange starts, which has no date.
- [INF] Software need: seller due-diligence data collection from 1 Jan 2027 and an annual XML report in early 2028, for Ukrainian platform operators. Large ones (OLX, Prom, Rozetka, Uklon, Bolt) build in-house. The number of small operators that would buy is [UNK] and probably in the low hundreds at most. Free state alternative: none yet.

**VAT on parcels under EUR 150.**

- [V] First bill failed in May 2026; re-submitted; two laws signed 22 Oct 2026; rules not earlier than 1 July 2027 [S45, S46, S47].
- [INF] The compliance burden sits with foreign marketplaces and postal operators. For Ukrainian small importers and dropshippers it is a price shock, not a software purchase.

**Reservation and military registration.**

- [V] 2026 brought new criteria and a transition: decisions on "critical" status valid on 2 June 2026 ran to at most 1 Sept 2026; firms had to re-confirm with a full document set (decision within 10 working days); salary threshold 25,941 UAH (three minimum wages), 21,618 UAH near the front; a person counts in one employer's quota only; on 8 Oct 2026 the economy ministry circulated a draft aimed at "employment schemes", and limits for defence and municipal firms were under discussion [S48, S49, S50, S51].
- [INF] If the three-minimum-wage rule holds and the draft 2027 minimum wage passes, the threshold rises to about 28,638 UAH on 1 Jan 2027, so every reserving employer has to re-check payroll in January.
- [INF] This is the one trigger that is live, recurring and affects many employers. [PRIOR, high] Submission itself runs through the Дія portal at no charge. See opportunity 5 for why it still ranks low.

### 4.3 What the trigger review means

- [INF] **Deadline risk in Ukraine is systematic, not incidental.** е-Акциз slipped twice (1 Jan 2026, then 1 Nov 2026, now 1 July 2027), VAT-for-ФОП changed shape three times in eight months and then left the year, е-ТТН slipped five months before reaching parliament, the SAF-T bill was withdrawn, the first parcel-VAT bill was voted down. A product whose revenue depends on a Ukrainian statutory date should be assumed to lose 6–12 months.
- [INF] Where a deadline does hold, the adoption flows to whoever already owns the till, the document channel or the accountant.

### 5.1 Poland — KSeF

Timeline and rules:

- [PRIOR, high confidence] Under the August 2025 amendment: from 1 Feb 2026 issuing through KSeF is mandatory for taxpayers with 2024 sales above PLN 200M, and everyone must receive through KSeF; from 1 Apr 2026 all other taxpayers; from 1 Jan 2027 the smallest (invoiced sales up to PLN 10,000 a month). Penalties for not using KSeF apply from 1 Jan 2027. Consumer invoices are optional. Foreign businesses without a fixed establishment in Poland are not obliged to issue through KSeF.
- [UNK] Whether these dates held through 2026 and how the roll-out went.

Tooling and prices:

- [PRIOR, high] Free state tools exist: Aplikacja Podatnika KSeF, e-mikrofirma inside e-Urząd Skarbowy, and a mobile KSeF app. The API is open and free, with a test environment.
- [PRIOR, medium] Commercial invoicing tools (Fakturownia, inFakt, wFirma, iFirma) sell invoicing tiers at roughly PLN 0–60 a month, with free tiers common. Accounting bureaus charge a sole proprietor roughly PLN 200–500 a month. Exact 2026 prices [UNK].
- [PRIOR, low to medium] inFakt launched a Ukrainian-language version in 2022 and Fakturownia supports several interface languages. Whether their KSeF flows and support work in Ukrainian today is [UNK], and it is the single most important fact to check for this niche.
- [V] Finmap already markets to Ukrainians "with business in Europe, income in PayPal, expenses in Revolut, taxes in Ukraine" [S71].

Ukrainian-owned sole proprietorships (JDG):

- [PRIOR, medium] The Polish Economic Institute counted about 59.8k JDG founded by Ukrainian citizens between January 2022 and mid-2024, about one in ten new registrations.
- [INF] At that run-rate cumulative registrations would be near 100k by late 2026. The share still active is [UNK].

Who still lacks tooling:

- [INF] The April 2026 wave covered almost every JDG that invoices other businesses, which includes the typical Ukrainian-owned one (IT contractor, construction subcontractor, driver, beauty services invoicing a salon). They have had tooling for seven months.
- [INF] The January 2027 wave is only those invoicing under PLN 10,000 a month: the smallest businesses, least able to pay.
- [INF] Penalties starting in 2027 create a second, weaker push among those who ignored KSeF during the penalty-free year.

Named niches from the brief:

- Foreign companies: [PRIOR] not obliged without a fixed establishment, so the need is weak.
- E-commerce platforms: [PRIOR] consumer invoices are optional and business invoices already flow through order-management integrators.
- Ukrainian-owned JDG: a language niche, probably partly served.

Friction for a Ukrainian ФОП selling into Poland:

## 7. The six most promising opportunities

Each uses the same fields. Funnel figures are planning assumptions, not benchmarks.

### Opportunity 1 — Proposal-ready CV and bench packaging for IT outstaffing/outsourcing firms

- **Who has the problem / pays / decides.** Sales, recruiting and delivery staff at IT service firms that sell people (outstaffing, staff augmentation, dedicated teams), 10–500 staff. Paid by card from the sales or operations budget. [ASM] Decided by the founder, COO or head of sales in firms under about 100 people; no procurement process at this price.
- **Recurring job and trigger.** Each client request needs 2–10 candidate CVs in the vendor's branded, anonymised format, tailored to the request, usually within hours. Trigger: every inbound request. Volume rises when the bench grows.
- **Current alternatives and prices.** Manual Word or Google Docs templates (free, slow). A ChatGPT or Claude seat at $20 per user per month plus manual layout — the real competitor. Flowcase at enterprise quotes [PRIOR]. Recruitment-agency formatters at roughly $30–100 per seat [PRIOR, low]. HRIS/ATS exports. No bank or state tool is relevant.
- **Measurable cost of the status quo.** [ASM] 30–60 minutes per CV × 20–100 CVs a month = 10–100 staff hours, plus slower submissions in a market where the first few vendors to respond get the interview. To be measured in discovery calls.
- **Evidence of willingness to pay.** [PRIOR] The category exists and charges per seat in staffing; Ukrainian agencies pay USD for sales tooling. [UNK] No direct evidence was found this session that Ukrainian outstaffing firms pay for CV formatting.
- **Wedge for a solo vendor.** Upload the firm's own DOCX template once and get output that matches it exactly; anonymisation rules; tailoring to the job description; Ukrainian/Russian-to-English rewriting; batch mode; a shareable bench catalogue. Sold and supported in Ukrainian, invoiced by a Ukrainian ФОП.
- **Onboarding and time to first value.** Self-serve. First formatted CV in under 10 minutes.
- **Reaching the first buyers.** [ASM] 1,500–3,000 Ukrainian IT service firms are listed on DOU, Clutch and LinkedIn. Outbound in Ukrainian to heads of sales, recruiters and resource managers at 30–50 messages a day, plus outstaffing/bench Telegram and Slack groups and IT-cluster communities. Cost: founder time and about $100 a month of LinkedIn tooling. [ASM] If 1–2% of contacted firms pay within 60 days, that is 15–30 Ukrainian customers by month 4. The rest must come from Poland, Romania, the Baltics, India and Latin America with the same playbook in English.
- **Retention.** The need lasts as long as the firm sells staff. Churn risk is substitution. Stickiness comes from stored templates, CV history and team seats.
- **Solo-operability and support.** High. Variable cost is cents per CV. Support concentrates in template fidelity, which must stay self-serve or it becomes per-client setup.
- **Integrations.** None required. Optional ATS/HRIS imports later. No КЕП, tax or bank API.
- **Price, ARPU, customers for $10k.** $49 to $149 per company; ARPU about $80; **about 125 customers**.
- **Wartime and market risks.** Mobilisation and flat IT exports shrink benches and budgets. LLM vendors keep making formatting easier. Ukraine alone is too small: 125 customers would be 4–8% of all such firms within a year.
- **Most dangerous assumption.** That firms will pay a subscription for output a general-purpose AI seat produces well enough.
- **Kill-test.** 15 discovery calls in week 1. Continue only if at least 5 firms can name the hours they spend per month and 3 pre-pay.

### Opportunity 2 — Prozorro AI bid assistant for SME suppliers

- **Who / pays / decides.** SMEs supplying the public sector. The owner or an in-house tender manager decides and pays from the company account; an invoice from a ФОП works.
- **Recurring job and trigger.** Find relevant tenders daily. For each: read the documentation, extract qualification requirements and required documents, assemble the pack, track deadlines and amendments, watch competitors. Trigger: every new tender in their categories.
- **Alternatives and prices.** Free search and alerts on accredited marketplaces [PRIOR]. Clarity Project analytics (paid; price [UNK]). Tender-support agencies per tender or on retainer (prices [UNK]). An in-house specialist. General AI chat. State: the Prozorro portal, BI Prozorro and DOZORRO are free [PRIOR].
- **Cost of the status quo.** Disqualification for a missing document after the bid fee is paid and the work done; specialist salary; hours per tender. Magnitude [UNK].
- **Evidence of willingness to pay.** [PRIOR] Suppliers already pay per-bid fees and agencies, and paid analytics has existed for years. Price points [UNK].
- **Wedge.** Requirement extraction into a checklist that cites the source clause; auto-drafted free-form declarations with company details; semantic rather than keyword alerts; amendment diffs; competitor price history from open data. It does not submit bids, so it needs no accreditation.
- **Onboarding and time to first value.** Enter the company code and categories; see matching live tenders with checklists within minutes.
- **Reaching the first buyers.** [PRIOR, medium] Bid and award records expose supplier contact points, so a targeted outbound list can be built from open data at no cost, for example firms disqualified in the last 90 days with the stated reason. Plus tender-specialist Facebook and Telegram groups and referral deals with tender agencies. [ASM] 20,000 contacts → 2% trials → 20% paid ≈ 80 customers; the remaining 220 need content and partners.
- **Retention.** High for regular bidders, poor for occasional ones.
- **Solo-operability and support.** Feasible. The risk is users treating output as legal advice; needs disclaimers and no appeal drafting.
- **Integrations.** Prozorro public API, open [PRIOR]. No КЕП.
- **Price, ARPU, customers for $10k.** 990–2,490 UAH a month; ARPU about 1,500 UAH; **about 300 customers**.
- **Risks.** Marketplaces add the same AI features free because they earn on bid fees. Non-defence budgets are cut in wartime. Procurement-law changes [UNK]. Revenue is in UAH.
- **Most dangerous assumption.** That small suppliers will pay about 1,500 UAH a month when alerts are free and many bid only occasionally.
- **Kill-test.** Build the disqualification list for one category, email 200 firms, and require 10 replies asking for the checklist.

### Opportunity 3 — ФОП-contractor operations for firms with 10–300 contractors

- **Who / pays / decides.** Companies running teams of ФОП contractors: IT firms, digital agencies, studios, outsourced sales and support. The chief accountant or finance lead uses it; the founder or CFO pays.
- **Recurring job and trigger.** Each month: collect hours or fixed fees, generate acts and invoices, obtain both signatures, build the bank payment file, archive. Also onboarding documents and watching each contractor's registration status, tax group and income limit. Triggers: every month-end, every new contractor, and rule changes (2027 limits [S27]; possible group 2/3 reform [S19, S22]).
- **Alternatives and prices.** Вчасно.ЕДО, Paperless or Dubidoc for signing [PRIOR; prices UNK]. HRIS such as PeopleForce and Hurma [PRIOR]. Spreadsheets and mail merges. Accounting outsourcers. Add-ons to accounting systems. Banks and state: nothing for the cycle itself; registries are open for status checks [PRIOR].
- **Cost of the status quo.** [ASM] 1–3 accountant days a month per 50–100 contractors, and audit exposure from unsigned or wrong acts.
- **Evidence of willingness to pay.** [PRIOR] Firms already pay for e-document exchange and HRIS. [PRIOR, medium] Foreign employers pay about $49 per contractor per month to contractor-management platforms for a heavier version of the job.
- **Wedge.** "Timesheet to signed act to payment file in ten minutes", with registry monitoring. Signing is delegated to an existing provider's API, not rebuilt.
- **Onboarding and time to first value.** A CSV of contractors and one template produce the first batch the same day.
- **Reaching the first buyers.** The same companies as opportunity 1, so the pipeline is shared. Finance contacts through LinkedIn, webinars with accounting portals, IT clusters. [ASM] 1,000 firms contacted → 5% demos → 30% close ≈ 15 customers per 1,000.
- **Retention.** High once month-end runs through it.
- **Solo-operability and support.** Medium. Contracts and acts differ per firm, which pulls toward per-client configuration. The founder is not a lawyer or accountant, so templates must be the customer's own.
- **Integrations.** An e-signature provider's API (availability and cost [UNK]); file-based bank imports; open registries. КЕП signing happens inside the provider. No tax e-cabinet integration.
- **Price, ARPU, customers for $10k.** About $2 per contractor per month with a $49 minimum; ARPU $100–150; **about 70–100 firms**.
- **Risks.** Migration from ФОП contracts to Diia.City gig contracts. Reform of the simplified system. An incumbent adds a module.
- **Most dangerous assumption.** That mid-size firms have not already solved this with Вчасно and a script.
- **Kill-test.** 10 calls with chief accountants; require 4 to describe more than a day a month and name what they spend now.

### Opportunity 4 — Ukrainian-language KSeF assistant for Ukrainian sole proprietors in Poland

- **Who / pays / decides.** Ukrainian citizens running a JDG in Poland with weak Polish. They pay themselves; the accountant often picks the tool.
- **Recurring job and trigger.** Issue invoices through KSeF, fix rejections, receive cost invoices, remember social-insurance and tax dates. Triggers: 1 Jan 2027 for the smallest; penalties from 2027 [PRIOR].
- **Alternatives and prices.** Free state apps. Polish invoicing tools at roughly PLN 0–60 [PRIOR]. Accounting bureaus at PLN 200–500, many Ukrainian-speaking [PRIOR].
- **Cost of the status quo.** The bureau fee; from 2027, penalties of up to 100% of the VAT on an invoice issued outside KSeF [PRIOR, high].
- **Evidence of willingness to pay.** They pay bureaus. For a separate tool [UNK].
- **Wedge.** Ukrainian/Russian interface and support; plain-language explanation of errors.
- **Onboarding and time to first value.** The customer generates a KSeF token or certificate; about 30 minutes. Test environment is open [PRIOR].
- **Reaching the first buyers.** Ukrainian-language Facebook, Telegram and TikTok communities in Poland; Ukrainian-speaking bureaus as resellers. Volumes and costs [UNK].
- **Retention.** Good if it becomes the invoicing tool.
- **Solo-operability and support.** Medium; tax questions arrive as support tickets.
- **Integrations.** KSeF API, open [PRIOR]. PLN payments through a merchant of record. No Polish entity.
- **Price, ARPU, customers for $10k.** PLN 29–49 ($8–13); **about 800–1,250 customers**.
- **Risks.** The main wave passed in April 2026. Incumbents may already work in Ukrainian. The state app is free. GDPR transfers. No legal presence.
- **Most dangerous assumption.** That incumbents do not already serve this group in Ukrainian.
- **Kill-test.** Check whether inFakt, Fakturownia and wFirma offer KSeF flows and support in Ukrainian. If any does, stop.

### Opportunity 5 — Military-registration and reservation compliance tracker for employers

- **Who / pays / decides.** Employers with staff liable for military service, especially those holding or seeking "critical" status. HR or the accountant uses it; the director pays.
- **Recurring job and trigger.** Keep the register, track reservation expiry, check the salary threshold monthly, re-confirm status when rules change. Triggers: several rule changes in 2026 [S48–S51]; a probable threshold rise on 1 Jan 2027 [INF].
- **Alternatives and prices.** Submission through Дія is free [PRIOR, high]. HRIS and accounting-system modules; content and webinars from the accounting and HR portals (prices [UNK]); spreadsheets.
- **Cost of the status quo.** Fines of 34,000–59,500 UAH [PRIOR, high] and loss of reserved staff.
- **Evidence of willingness to pay.** [UNK]. The accounting and HR portals cover the topic heavily with Q&A articles and webinars [S49], which shows attention, not spend.
- **Wedge.** Threshold and expiry monitoring with ready-to-upload lists.
- **Onboarding and time to first value.** Upload a payroll export; same day.
- **Reaching the first buyers.** Accounting and HR portals and groups. Volumes and costs [UNK].
- **Retention.** Lasts as long as mobilisation does.
- **Solo-operability and support.** Technically feasible. But the product would hold lists of mobilisation-liable people with salaries: a high-value target in wartime and a personal-data liability for a sole proprietor.
- **Integrations.** None available; state registers have no third-party API [PRIOR].
- **Price, ARPU, customers for $10k.** 500–1,500 UAH; **300–900 customers**.
- **Risks.** The need disappears with a ceasefire. The state keeps automating. A breach would be catastrophic.
- **Most dangerous assumption.** That employers will upload this data to an unknown solo vendor.
- **Kill-test.** Ask 10 HR leads or accountants whether they would upload the list. If fewer than 5 say yes, stop.

- **Retention.** High; annual cycle.
- **Solo-operability and support.** Feasible.
- **Integrations.** The report format has not been published [UNK].

## 8. Verdict and ranking

**Is any local or CEE opportunity a better bet for $10k MRR in 12 months than a global English-language B2B product? No.**

- A UAH-priced product for Ukrainian micro-business needs 1,300–4,300 customers at prevailing prices, against free state and bank tools and consolidating incumbents, with no dependable forcing event before 2028.
- A CEE compliance product has more reliable deadlines but local incumbents, free state apps and low prices, and the founder's legal and payment advantages do not cross the border.
- The opportunities that survive (1 and 3, and to a degree 2) do so because the buyer has a company budget and the price is $50–150 or 1,500+ UAH. Opportunity 1 is, in substance, a global product that happens to start at home.

Ranking, with my subjective odds of reaching $10k MRR by month 12 (analyst judgment, not derived from data):

| Rank | Opportunity (number in section 7) | Customers needed | Subjective odds | Evidence quality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CV and bench packaging for IT outstaffing firms, Ukraine first then global (1) | ~125 at $80 | 10–15% | Structural reasoning; demand unverified |
| 2 | Prozorro AI bid assistant (2) | ~300 at 1,500 UAH | 5–10% | Structural reasoning; prices unverified |
| 3 | ФОП-contractor operations (3) | ~70–100 at $100–150 | 5–8% | Structural reasoning; incumbents unverified |
| 4 | Reservation/military-registration tracker (5) | 300–900 | 3–5% | Trigger verified; data risk disqualifying |
| 5 | KSeF assistant for Ukrainians in Poland (4) | 800–1,250 | 2–4% | Unverified; timing and ARPU against it |
| 6 | DAC7 kit for small platforms (6) | 25–70 | 1–3% | Law verified; buyer count unknown |

For calibration: I would put a generic, well-chosen global English B2B micro-SaaS by the same founder in the same 8–15% band as opportunity 1. The home-field beachhead does not raise the ceiling; it shortens the time to the first 20 customers.

What would change this verdict:

- A signed law fixing VAT for simplified ФОП with a firm 2027 date and no free pre-filling (currently the opposite on both counts).
- Proof that Polish incumbents do not serve Ukrainian-speaking JDG owners and that tens of thousands of them fall in the January 2027 wave.
- Proof that Prozorro suppliers already pay 1,500+ UAH a month for comparable tools.

### Cross-cutting wartime and market risks

- **Policy volatility.** [V] See 4.3: dates move, and plans reverse within months.
- **Customer-base turnover.** [V] 102,258 ФОП closed in H1 2026 against 141,472 opened [S4]. [INF] That is roughly a 9% annualised closure rate on the 2.18M base, a floor under churn for any ФОП-facing product.
- **Blackouts.** [V] Still an operating condition in 2026; Checkbox publishes blackout workarounds for its registers [S72]. Hosting abroad protects the product, not customers' trading hours or the founder's working day.
- **Currency.** [V] 45.50 UAH/USD against the brief's 42 [S1]. UAH price lists need regular increases.
- **Reachable base.** [V] 146,479 ФОП are registered in occupied territories [S9].
- **Founder continuity.** [UNK] A one-person business in Ukraine has a single point of failure (mobilisation, relocation, outages). The brief does not state the founder's status; the selection team should ask.
