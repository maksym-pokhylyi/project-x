Construction

One fold makes the tick.

Fold the top-left corner of a 3 : 4 sheet down along a line 22.5° off the top edge. Its two edges land at 45° and form a tick with arms in the ratio 1 : 2.41. Nothing is drawn on; the tick is the gap the fold leaves.
