Billing questions

Something else? Write to hello@bidfolio.co.uk.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Prices exclude VAT. VAT is added at checkout where it applies.

What does annual billing save?

Annual billing gives two months free. Radar Pro is £290 a year instead of £348, Bid Desk £1,490 instead of £1,788, and Bid Desk Plus £3,490 instead of £4,188. We email a reminder seven days before an annual plan renews.

How do I pause or cancel?

Pause or cancel in two clicks from Billing. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Your first subscription payment, or an unused Bid Pass, is refunded on request within 14 days. After that, cancel from Billing and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

What counts as a full bid?

One tender taken from the buyer’s pack to a marked first draft: the cited check, the scored-question matrix, first drafts from your documents, marking against the buyer’s scheme and Word export. Bid Desk includes 3 full bids a month and Bid Desk Plus 10. Unused bids roll over one month.

What is a Bid Pass?

One full bid for £99, paid once, with no subscription. It adds one full bid to any plan, including Free, so it suits firms that bid a few times a year. An unused Bid Pass is refunded on request within 14 days.

How are payments taken?

Payments are processed by Paddle. Subscriptions are billed monthly or annually, and a Bid Pass is a single one-off payment. VAT is added at checkout where it applies.
