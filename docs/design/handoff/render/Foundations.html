The five rules

01

Amber marks, amber ink writes

Amber #B4690E is for markers, rules and numerals of 24 px and up; it never carries small text. Small deadline text uses amber ink #925207, which is 5.76 : 1 on paper and 5.19 : 1 on amber tint.

Closes 27 Nov 2026 14 days left 14days
02

One green

“Met” and “passed” are brand green with a tick; the separate success colour is retired. An action is a filled button and a status is a ticked label, so the two are told apart by form, not hue.

Met Start a bid check
03

Line strong is for form controls

Line strong #857F70 (3.99 : 1 on white) edges inputs, tick boxes and outline buttons, and nothing else. Dividers and card edges stay on line #E3DED2.

Company name Save
04

Links are ink, underlined green

Info blue #1F4F8F never marks a link. It only labels neutral facts, such as a notice type or a point that is for you to confirm, and always with words beside it.

05

Fonts from bidfolio.co.uk, figures tabular

The build serves all three families itself. This canvas has to load them from Google Fonts, so treat that as a property of the file, not of the product. Tables use Inter’s tabular figures so pounds and dates line up.
