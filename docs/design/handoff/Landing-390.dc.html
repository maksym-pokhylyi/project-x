Questions

What suppliers ask first

Where does the information on my page come from?

From notices that UK public bodies publish openly: tender, award and contract notices on Find a Tender and Contracts Finder. We match them to your company by name and Companies House number. If a notice is wrong or missing, you can flag it from your page.

Is Bidfolio part of GOV.UK?

No. Bidfolio is an independent company. We use public notices under the Open Government Licence and have no connection to the Cabinet Office, Find a Tender or Contracts Finder. You still submit your bid through the buyer’s own portal.

Will the drafts invent things about my company?

No. Drafts are written only from documents you upload, and each claim carries a marker back to the document and page it came from. Where your documents do not support a point the question asks for, the draft leaves a labelled gap for you to fill instead of guessing.

Does it write the whole bid for me?

It gives you a compliant first draft and an honest mark. Pricing, the final wording and the decision to submit stay with you.
