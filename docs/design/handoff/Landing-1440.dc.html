What suppliers ask first

Is this an official government service?

No. Bidfolio is an independent service. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the UK Government. The notices come from the Find a Tender service and are reused under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Where are my documents kept, and who can see them?

Your tender packs and company documents are stored in the EU (Frankfurt) and encrypted in transit and at rest. Only people in your workspace can open them. They are never used to train AI models: we send them to AI providers only under zero-data-retention terms, so providers do not keep them after answering. You can delete a bid, a document or your whole workspace at any time, and the files are removed from our storage.

Does AI write my bid?

It writes a first draft using only the documents you upload, shows where every claim comes from, and marks gaps instead of filling them. You edit, decide and submit. If a buyer asks whether AI was used, say so — public buyers allow AI-assisted bids.

How do I cancel?

Pause or cancel in two clicks from Billing. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. We email a reminder seven days before an annual plan renews. Your first subscription payment, or an unused Bid Pass, is refunded on request within 14 days.
